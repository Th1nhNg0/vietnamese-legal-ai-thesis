## About This Thesis

This was my bachelor thesis in Data Science, where I explored how modern AI systems, especially large language models and retrieval-based methods, can be applied to the legal domain.

The main motivation came from a simple idea: **law should be easier to understand and access**.

In Vietnam, many people see legal documents as difficult, dry, and intimidating. However, law affects everyday life, and better tools can help ordinary users, law students, and legal professionals search, understand, and work with legal information more effectively.

In this thesis, I researched the opportunities and challenges of applying deep learning to legal systems, then built experiments for retrieving Vietnamese legal documents using traditional information retrieval methods and embedding-based search.
